## About This Dashboard

This dashboard lets you explore the results of the LAC Government Analytics
Survey — MIS Questionnaires, economy by economy: compare an economy against
the LAC average for each Management Information System (MIS) module, browse
the underlying free-text responses, and generate an automated economy brief
summarizing the survey results.

*The survey responses shown throughout this dashboard reflect the views and
self-reported information of the participating governments and respondents.
They do not represent the views, position, or endorsement of the World Bank
Group, its Board of Executive Directors, or the governments they represent.*

## The Survey

The survey covers two questionnaires:

- **MIS modules** (Human Resources, Procurement, Public Financial
  Management, Tax, Education, Health): institutional arrangements, use of
  data analytics, funding, collaboration, and data governance around each
  system.
- **Capabilities**: government-wide analytical capacity-building (dedicated
  career tracks, skills assessments, training, and capacity initiatives),
  independent of any single MIS.
