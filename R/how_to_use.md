### How to use this dashboard

**1. Select a country**
Use the **Country** dropdown in the left sidebar to choose the country you want to explore. All charts and text responses update automatically based on your selection.

**2. Check the country's coverage**
Below the country selector, two badges summarize what data is available:
- **Income group**: the World Bank income classification for the selected country.
- **MIS modules**: how many of the six MIS modules the country answered, and whether it also completed the Capabilities questionnaire.

**3. Choose a questionnaire**
The **Questionnaire** panel lists the seven available modules:
- Human Resources MIS
- Procurement MIS
- Public Financial MIS
- Tax MIS
- Education MIS
- Health MIS
- Capabilities

Select one to view its results for the chosen country. If the country did not answer a particular module, the dashboard will let you know instead of showing empty charts.

**4. Choose a view**
For the six MIS modules, you can toggle between:
- **Country only**: shows only the selected country's responses.
- **vs. LAC average**: compares the country's responses against the average of all countries that answered that module. This is the default view.

(This toggle is not available for Capabilities, since it does not have module-level peer comparisons in the same way.)

**5. Explore the charts**
The **Explore charts** tab shows one chart per survey question. In the comparison view:
- Bars show the percentage of countries selecting each response category.
- A blue diamond (◆) marks the category the selected country chose.
- Each chart can be downloaded individually as a PNG using the **Download PNG** button below it.

**6. Read the text responses**
The **Text responses** tab shows the free-text comments countries provided for each question, translated into English when needed, organized by question.

**7. Learn more about the survey**
The **About the survey** tab provides background on the LAC Government Analytics Survey: its purpose, scope, and methodology.
