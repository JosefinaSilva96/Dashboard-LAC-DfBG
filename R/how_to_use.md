## How to Use This Dashboard

1. **Select an economy** in the sidebar. The badges below the selector show
   its income group, how many MIS modules it answered, and whether it
   answered the Capabilities questionnaire.
2. **Select a questionnaire**: one of the six MIS modules, or Capabilities.
3. **Explore charts**: each chart shows the selected economy's answer
   compared to the LAC average for economies that answered the same
   questionnaire (or module). Switch to "Country only" to see just that
   economy's answer.
4. **Text responses**: browse the free-text comments respondents left to
   support their answers.
5. **Download economy brief**: generates a short .docx summarizing the
   selected economy's answers for the currently selected questionnaire.
