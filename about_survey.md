### About the survey

This dashboard presents results from the **LAC Government Analytics Survey**, the data collection effort behind the World Bank report *"Data for Better Governance: Building Government Analytics Ecosystems in Latin America and the Caribbean"* (November 2024).

The survey covers six management information systems (MIS) commonly used across the public sector — Human Resources, Procurement, Public Financial Management, Tax, Education, and Health — plus a separate module on government data analytics **Capabilities**. For each MIS module, it captures how digitized the system is, how the data it generates is used, and the barriers governments face in moving from descriptive reporting toward more advanced analytics.

The underlying research finds that public administrations across the region have widely adopted these information systems — by 2022 nearly all countries had public finance and tax MIS in place, most had a human resources MIS, and the majority had e-procurement systems — but that the data these systems generate is still used almost exclusively for basic descriptive reporting rather than for decision-making. The report also highlights that many of these systems remain only partially digitized, that data quality and integration mechanisms are uneven across the region, and that governments have limited dedicated career paths for data analytics staff, all of which constrain the shift toward more advanced, decision-oriented use of government data.

You can read the full report here:
[Data for Better Governance: Building Government Analytics Ecosystems in Latin America and the Caribbean](https://www.worldbank.org/en/region/lac/publication/data-for-better-governance-building-government-analytics-ecosystems-in-latin-america-and-the-caribbean)
